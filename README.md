# accountability-buddy-sidekick
Helps people keep focus so they progress as they are meant to

## The app

`index.html` is Accountability Buddy, published as a claude.ai artifact. It uses the Sidekick design system.

- **Home**: the time and date, steps done, goals done, minutes left, the next step, and a meter for how much of today is done.
- **Goals**: today's goals as cards, filtered by All, To do and Done.
- **Steps**: the small steps of one goal, each with its estimated minutes and its status (Not started, In progress, Done).

On claude.ai, data is saved to the viewer's account. Opened as a plain file, it is saved in that browser only.
